# Acceptance Checklist

Before finishing, confirm every item:

- The page opens without a build step.
- The supplied background fills the viewport without black bars.
- No central card, panel, or box is visible.
- The timer is readable but does not obscure the scene.
- Preset buttons update the countdown.
- Custom input accepts 1–180 and has no spinner arrows.
- Start changes the timer every second.
- Pause and continue work.
- End asks for confirmation and records complete elapsed minutes.
- Natural completion records one completed session.
- Reloading preserves the active timer and today's totals.
- The browser console has no errors.
- The layout works at desktop and phone widths.
